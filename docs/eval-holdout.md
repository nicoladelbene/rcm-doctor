## Methodology

Company: Limmatquell Privatkliniken AG (Private hospital and outpatient clinic network), fictional. Controls and ground-truth labels were generated by `openai/gpt-5.6-sol`
in one call, with a labeling guide written independently of the review prompt, and committed before any review ran.
The reviewer configuration (prompt and rules; SHA-256 in `data/holdout/`) was frozen before the holdout was generated, unless the title says the set was seen.
Labels were not edited after seeing results, so disagreements include label noise from the generator as well as reviewer errors.
