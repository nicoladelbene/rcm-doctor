## Variant A — result hook

> I gave an AI a 40-control RCM with 33 planted defects. It found all 33, and it didn't flag a single clean control.
> Then I had a different AI write 30 new controls it had never seen, a third of them in German. Score: 82%. In English it holds up; in German it doesn't yet, and that's next on the list.
> Every finding points to the exact words behind it, and each weak control gets a rewrite you can actually test.
> Open source, and the demo needs no API key. Link in the first comment.
> Week 1 of a weekend series on AI for internal controls. Next update in December.

## Variant B — problem hook

> "Finance ensures that supplier invoices are checked and are correct."
> Every IKS has controls like this one: no owner, no trigger, no evidence, no threshold.
> rcm-doctor reviews each control like a senior controls reviewer would, cites the text and proposes a rewrite.
> Measured against ground truth: 100% recall on the 40-control demo set, and 82% on an independent holdout written by a different model (German is the known weak spot).
> Demo and code are in the first comment. This is Week 1 of a series.
